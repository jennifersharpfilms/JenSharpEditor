About

Cutting stories that matter.

Jennifer Sharp is an award-winning film and video editor based in Los Angeles. She specializes in narrative and documentary features, series, and web content — bringing a deep commitment to performance, pacing, and emotional truth to every project.

Her work spans comedy, drama, and documentary, with a track record of helping filmmakers realize their vision from the cutting room. Guild Eligible — Motion Picture Editors Guild, Local 700. Fluent in Spanish.
